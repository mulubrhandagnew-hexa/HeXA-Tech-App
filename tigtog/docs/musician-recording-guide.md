# Tigtog — recording guide for musicians

Thank you for helping Tigtog sound like real Tigray music. All you need is
a phone. Please read the whole page once before you start.

## Before you record

- **Quiet room.** No TV, music, fans or people talking.
- **Phone about 50 cm–1 m from the instrument**, standing still (on a table
  or stand, not in your hand).
- **Use the phone's normal voice/audio recorder** (not a video, not WhatsApp
  voice notes — they reduce quality). Any format is fine (m4a, mp3, wav).
- **One file per recording.** Name it like: `kebero_guayla_88bpm_take1`.
- **Traditional only:** play traditional Tigray ጓይላ / wedding / dance
  patterns, as you would at a ወርሓ or ሰርግ — not modern, Western or
  reggae beats, and not someone else's recorded song.

## 1. ከበሮ (kebero)

**A. Single hits** (two files):
- `kebero_dum`: 10–15 deep, open strokes in the middle of the big head ("DUM").
- `kebero_ta`: 10–15 sharp strokes near the edge / small head ("ta").
- About **one second of silence between hits**; let each one ring.
- Play some softer, some harder.

**B. ጓይላ loops** (several files):
- Play a traditional ጓይላ pattern steadily for **20–40 seconds**.
- Record at least: a **slow** one, a **medium** (wedding) one, and a **fast** one.
- If you know other traditional Tigray rhythms (e.g. for different dances or
  regions), record each one and **tell us its name**.

## 2. ኽራር (krar)

**A. Tuning:** pluck **each string once**, from lowest to highest, with 2
seconds between strings. Then say the name of the ቕኒት you tuned to
(e.g. ትዝታ) at the end of the recording.

**B. Riffs / loops:** play typical traditional ጓይላ ኽራር patterns for
**20–40 seconds** each, in the same ቕኒት. 3–6 different patterns.

**C. Optional:** the same patterns played together with the ከበሮ, if two
players are available.

## 3. ዋጣ / ሸምበቆ / voice (optional)

Short traditional phrases (10–30 seconds each). Singers: traditional songs
only (not another artist's song), or your own.

## For each file, tell us

- Instrument, rhythm/dance name, and roughly how fast (slow / medium / fast)
- ቕኒት (for ኽራር / ዋጣ)
- Your name as you'd like to be credited (or "anonymous")

## Sending

Send the files (and the signed permission below) to the Tigtog team by
email, WhatsApp document, or Google Drive link.
